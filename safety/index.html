Field practices

How our crews work on every route, before the first tool comes out and until the site is closed.

  • Job hazard analysis before work starts

    The crew reviews the tasks, the hazards and the controls for that site before anyone starts work.

  • Stop-work authority for every crew member

    Anyone on the crew can stop the work when something looks unsafe. Work restarts once the hazard is fixed.

  • 811 locates before any dig

    We call 811 and confirm utilities are marked before any bore or excavation.

  • Traffic control set up to plan

    Work zones follow the approved traffic control plan, to protect crews, drivers and pedestrians.
