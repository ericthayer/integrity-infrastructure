Questions about our scope

Do you install internet service inside homes?

No. We build fiber up to residential locations, and the internet provider handles installation inside the home.

Can one contractor handle the whole route?

Yes. Underground construction, aerial work, splicing and restoration are all available from one contractor, so one company is accountable for the route, from the backbone to the residential location.

Do you offer maintenance and emergency repair?

Yes. We provide scheduled maintenance and emergency repair so your infrastructure stays operational and reliable.

Which states do you work in?

Our offices are in Sherwood, Oregon and Centennial, Colorado. Licensed states are listed under Where we work as they are confirmed.

What do you need for a quote?

The scope of work, the approximate footage, the project location, your timing and the site conditions, such as road crossings or surfaces to restore. Send them through the quote form, or call (503) 502-5339.
