Roles we hire

Field crews build the network. Office teams plan it, schedule it and keep it moving.

  • Fiber splicing technicians

    Splice and test fiber in closures, cabinets and splice trays.

  • Directional drill operators

    Run the drill that bores conduit under roads, yards and driveways.

  • Aerial lineworkers

    Hang strand and fiber on utility poles, often from a bucket truck.

  • Laborers and crew members

    Support every crew with conduit, materials, site setup and restoration.

  • CDL drivers

    Haul equipment and material to job sites and keep crews supplied.

  • Foremen and supervisors

    Lead crews in the field, plan the day and coach the people on it.

  • Office and project staff

    Plan, schedule, bill and support the work from Oregon and Colorado.
