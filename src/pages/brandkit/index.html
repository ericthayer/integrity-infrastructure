Four directions, one brand core

Every board uses the same brand: Montserrat, brand orange #E87722, ink #171717, the flame logo and the crew’s own photography. What changes is the expression: surface, type treatment, corners, rhythm, image grade and motion. Work Light and As-Built are the primary pair; Crew and Lit Fiber are the backups.
