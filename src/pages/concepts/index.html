Redesign concepts

Four directions on one brand core and one component system. Each Home page is built only from the design system; switching concepts changes tokens, section variants and motion, not components. Work Light and As-Built are the primary pair; Crew and Lit Fiber are the two backups, one for the deck.

  • Primary

    Work Light

    Dark and cinematic. Orange is the light on the job site. Closest evolution of today’s site.

    Home page

  • Secondary

    As-Built

    Light and engineered. Facts set like a title block, for PE buyers and bid packets.

    Home page

  • Alternate

    Crew

    Light and people-first. One orange field, crew photography, the Ways of BEING up front.

    Home page

  • Motion-led

    Lit Fiber

    Dark glass and light in motion: a WebGL strand scene, an island nav, service tiles that morph into sheets, a card stack and a values rail. Reads like an application.
