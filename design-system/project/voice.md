# Voice and copy

Integrity Infrastructure sounds like a foreman who respects your time: direct, specific, proud of the work without bragging about it.

## Words the company already owns

- **"Doing what's right when nobody is looking."**: the mantra. Always in quotes when quoted; on the website it is the About page title and the Home values section title.
- **Ways of BEING**: be generous, be accountable, be coachable, be safe, be caring, always be better. Keep BEING in capitals.
- **Build by building people**: a working tagline until the client confirms the wording. It appears only as the Careers page headline and the About mission title. Never the footer line, never a Home hero.
- Service names, in this order: Long-haul fiber construction, Fiber splicing, Boring, Aerial installations, Concrete and asphalt restoration.
- Footer brand line: "Fiber and utility construction, from long-haul routes to residential locations."

## Rules

- Lead with the work: "Underground utility installation with boring methods that limit disruption", not "We are passionate about infrastructure".
- Say where the work stops: we build long-haul fiber and the infrastructure that reaches residential locations, "to the home, never inside it". Never write "ISP installation" or describe installing service inside homes.
- The company is independent, with its own leadership, crews and offices. Never use the old line "An Integrity Corps company", never name Integrity Corps, Integrity Traffic or Integrity Equipment Services, and never link to their profiles.
- Short sentences. One idea each. Hero subtext stays at 20 words or fewer; section intros at 25.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons in the copy itself. Work Light's capitals come from CSS, so the words stay readable when styles are off.
- Numbers only when they are real and sourced. Anything the client has not confirmed renders as "Client to provide", never as a plausible guess.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a period, a comma, a colon or parentheses. Ranges use a hyphen or "to" ("3 to 6 months").
- No filler verbs (elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, revolutionize) and no invented slogans.
- Name places plainly: Sherwood, Oregon and Centennial, Colorado. No weather, time or locale strips.

## Forms and errors

- Labels are nouns ("Project location"); hints say what good input looks like ("City and state, or cross streets").
- Errors say how to fix it: "Enter an email address like name@company.com", "Enter the project location, like Sherwood, OR", "Choose at least one trade".
- Success says what happens next: "Thanks, your request is in. We will review the details and follow up."
