# Voice and copy

Integrity Infrastructure sounds like a foreman who respects your time: direct, specific, proud of the work without bragging about it.

## Words the company already owns

- **Build by Building People**: the tagline. Crew uses it as its hero; everywhere else it is the footer line.
- **"Doing what's right when nobody is looking."**: the mantra. Always in quotes when quoted; Work Light and As-Built use it as the values section title.
- **Ways of BEING**: be generous, be accountable, be coachable, be safe, be caring, always be better. Keep BEING in capitals.
- Service names: Fiber splicing, Boring, Aerial installations, ISP installation, Concrete and asphalt restoration.
- "An Integrity Corps company."

## Rules

- Lead with the work: "Underground utility installation with boring methods that limit disruption", not "We are passionate about infrastructure".
- Short sentences. One idea each. Hero subtext stays at 20 words or fewer; section intros at 25.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons in the copy itself. Work Light's capitals come from CSS, so the words stay readable when styles are off.
- Numbers only when they are real and sourced. Anything the client has not confirmed renders as "Client to provide", never as a plausible guess.
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a period, a comma, a colon or parentheses. Ranges use a hyphen or "to" ("3 to 6 months").
- No filler verbs (elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, revolutionize) and no invented slogans.
- Name places plainly: Sherwood, Oregon and Centennial, Colorado. No weather, time or locale strips.

## Action labels

| Intent | Label | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Quote | Request a quote | Contact page, `#quote` |
| Call | Call (503) 502-5339, or the number alone | `tel:+15035025339` |
| Careers | View open roles | Careers page, open roles |
| Partner | Start an inquiry | Subcontractor Inquiry page |
| Learn | Our services / See our safety program / About Integrity | The page named |

One label per intent across the whole site. "Get a quote", "Contact us for a quote" and "Get in touch" are all retired in favor of the labels above.

## Forms and errors

- Labels are nouns ("Project location"); hints say what good input looks like ("City and state, or the nearest cross streets").
- Errors say how to fix it: "Enter an email address like name@company.com", "Choose at least one service".
- Success says what happens next: "Thanks, your request is in. We will review the details and follow up."
