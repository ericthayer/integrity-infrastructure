# SiteFooter

The footer: brand line, company links, both offices, phone, email, social links and the licenses line, on the sunken surface in every concept.

## Use
- Oregon: 13565 SW Tualatin Sherwood Road, Suite 800, Sherwood, OR 97140. Colorado: 8085 S Chester St, Suite 220, Centennial, CO 80112. Phone (503) 502-5339, info@integrityinfrastructure.com.
- Brand line: "Fiber and utility construction, from long-haul routes to residential locations." No tagline and no parent-company line.
- Company links: Services, Safety, About, Careers, Subcontractors, Contact.
- Social links are icon buttons with `aria-label` ("Integrity Infrastructure on LinkedIn", "... on Instagram"): LinkedIn and Instagram only. The live site's Facebook icon pointed to another company's page and is removed.
- Legal row: the copyright line and "Contractor licenses:" with `ii-pending` until the client supplies the numbers.
- The year renders from `data-ii-year`.

## Consumer provides
Link lists, office addresses, contact details and social profiles.

## WordPress
Footer template; bind addresses and phone to site options.

## Avoid
Weather, time or locale strips, a second quote form, and inverting the footer to a different theme.
