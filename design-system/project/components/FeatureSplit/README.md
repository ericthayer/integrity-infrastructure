# FeatureSplit

A photo and copy side by side for a single idea: why Integrity, safety, joining the crew.

## Use
- `ii-split` with `ii-split__media` and `ii-split__content`; `ii-split--reverse` puts the photo on the right on desktop; `ii-split--wide-media` gives the photo more room.
- Content: heading, lead, then either checks (`ii-checks`), reasons, or one arrow link.
- No more than two splits in a row on a page; break the pattern with a full-width section.

## Consumer provides
Heading, lead, a photo (or PhotoSlot), and a list or link.

## WordPress
A two-column reusable block with a reverse toggle.

## Avoid
A third consecutive split, and small text over the photo.
