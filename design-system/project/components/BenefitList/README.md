# BenefitList

What working at Integrity offers, as icon, title and one sentence, in two columns.

## Use
- `ul.ii-benefits` of `li.ii-benefit`. True today: training and coaching (Careers carries the working tagline "Build by building people" as its headline), safety comes first, values you can see. Pay and benefits stay `ii-pending` until HR confirms them.

## Consumer provides
Benefit titles and sentences approved by HR.

## WordPress
A repeater field bound to a reusable block.

## Avoid
Generic perks the company has not confirmed.
