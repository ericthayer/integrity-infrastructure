# Timeline

Milestones in order, for company history on About (and as a step list for processes).

## Use
- `ol.ii-timeline` of `li.ii-timeline__item` with a year (`ii-timeline__year`) or a pending marker, a short title and one sentence.
- Years the client has not confirmed stay `ii-pending`.

## Consumer provides
Year, title and one sentence per milestone.

## WordPress
A repeater field bound to a reusable block.

## Avoid
Invented dates or events, and generic "Step 1" labels when used as a process (the step name is the label).
