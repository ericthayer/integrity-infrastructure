Services

The whole route, one team

Five service lines, from the long-haul route to the final restoration. Open any line for its scope. We build to the home, never inside it.

  1. Long-haul fiber construction

    Backbone and middle-mile fiber routes built underground and aerial, then carried to the residential locations they serve.

    • Long-haul and middle-mile routes
    • Distribution to residential locations
    • To the home, never inside it

  2. Fiber splicing

    Single-mode and multi-mode splicing that minimizes signal loss and keeps networks reliable.

    • Precision splicing for uninterrupted data transmission
    • Experienced technicians using current splicing equipment
    • Telecom, data center and commercial projects

  3. Boring

    Underground utility installation with boring methods that limit disruption and keep projects on schedule.

    • Fiber optic, water, gas and power lines
    • Non-invasive methods that protect the surrounding site
    • Urban, suburban and remote projects

  4. Aerial installations

    Secure, compliant aerial installations that help telecom and utility companies expand coverage.

    • Cost-effective for rural and urban areas
    • Weather-resistant installations built for the long term
    • Pole placement, cabling and maintenance

  5. Concrete and asphalt restoration

    When the underground work is done, we restore roads, driveways and sidewalks to their original condition.

    • Roads, driveways and sidewalks
    • Professional-grade materials and methods
    • Compliant with local standards and regulations

    Frequently asked questions

    What industries do you serve?

    We work with telecommunications companies, utilities, construction firms, government agencies and private businesses.

    Do you install internet service inside homes?

    No. We build the network up to residential locations, and the internet provider handles installation inside the home.

    Do you offer maintenance and emergency repair?

    Yes. We provide scheduled maintenance and emergency repair so your infrastructure stays operational and reliable.

    Where do you work?

    Our offices are in Sherwood, Oregon and Centennial, Colorado, and the coverage map shows the states we work in. Tell us where your project is and we will confirm coverage.

    How do I start a project with you?
