What we build

High-quality, efficient infrastructure work, from the first splice to the final restoration.

  1. Fiber splicing

    Single-mode and multi-mode splicing that minimizes signal loss and keeps networks reliable.

    • Precision splicing for uninterrupted data transmission
    • Experienced technicians using current splicing equipment
    • Telecom, data center and commercial projects

  2. Boring

    Underground utility installation with boring methods that limit disruption and keep projects on schedule.

    • Fiber optic, water, gas and power lines
    • Non-invasive methods that protect the surrounding site
    • Urban, suburban and remote projects

  3. Aerial installations

    Secure, compliant aerial installations that help telecom and utility companies expand coverage.

    • Cost-effective for rural and urban areas
    • Weather-resistant installations built for the long term
    • Pole placement, cabling and maintenance

  4. ISP installation

    Residential and commercial ISP installation for fast, reliable internet connectivity.

    • Installation for high-speed internet service
    • Scaled to each property and project
    • Ongoing support for network performance

  5. Concrete and asphalt restoration

    When the underground work is done, we restore roads, driveways and sidewalks to their original condition.

    • Roads, driveways and sidewalks
    • Professional-grade materials and methods
    • Compliant with local standards and regulations
